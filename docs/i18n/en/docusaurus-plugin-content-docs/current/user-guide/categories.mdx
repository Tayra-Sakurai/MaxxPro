# Category Management (Categories)

MaxxPro organizes inventory with a structured **3-level hierarchy (Large > Medium > Small)**.

---

## Hierarchy Overview

```mermaid
graph TD
    Large["Large Category<br/>e.g. Emergency Food"]
    Medium1["Medium Category<br/>e.g. Staples & Rice"]
    Medium2["Medium Category<br/>e.g. Canned & Sides"]
    Small1["Small Category<br/>e.g. Alpha Rice"]
    Small2["Small Category<br/>e.g. Canned Tuna"]
    Item1["Item<br/>Seaweed Rice 100g"]
    Item2["Item<br/>Tuna Flakes in Oil"]

    Large --> Medium1
    Large --> Medium2
    Medium1 --> Small1
    Medium2 --> Small2
    Small1 --> Item1
    Small2 --> Item2
```

- **Large Category**: High-level grouping (e.g., Food & Drink, Daily Goods, Medical, Power & Fuel).
- **Medium Category**: Subgroup under a Large Category (e.g., Canned Goods, Dry Food, Batteries).
- **Small Category**: Granular group. **Items link directly to Small Categories.**
