We are excited to announce the release of **MaxxPro 1.0**, a local-first stockpile management desktop application designed for Windows 11!

MaxxPro was created to make emergency preparedness and daily household inventory tracking reliable, private, and effortless. Adhering to a strict **Local-First** architecture, MaxxPro stores all records on your local machine with zero dependency on online databases or cloud services.

{/* truncate */}

## Motivation: Why Local-First?

During natural disasters (earthquakes, storms, power blackouts), network connections often fail. Your emergency supply checklist must be accessible at all times, without relying on external cloud infrastructure.

Additionally, household stockpiles, medical supplies, and storage spots are deeply personal data. With MaxxPro, your inventory remains private and completely under your control.

---

## Key Highlights

### 1. 100% Local-First & Zero-Cloud
MaxxPro uses Entity Framework Core 10 and SQLite (`Caiman.db`). All features operate flawlessly without an active internet connection.

### 2. 3-Tier Category Tree & Storage Places
- Organize items with **Large > Medium > Small** categories.
- Monitor storage locations (pantry, go bag, garage, vehicle) and track expiration dates with ease.

### 3. Local AI Assistant "Cougar"
Integrated with **Ollama** (`gemma4:e2b` / `embeddinggemma`) and `sqlite-vec`, Cougar allows you to query your inventory with natural language. Safety is guaranteed by **Human-in-the-Loop tool approvals** for all database modifications.

### 4. Modern Windows 11 Fluent Design
Built on WinUI 3 and Windows App SDK 1.7 with Mica material backdrop, dark/light theme support, and full bilingual localization.
